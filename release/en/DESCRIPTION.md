TITLE: The Fall of the Soviet Union (1985–1991) | How a Superpower Ended

On 25 December 1991 the Soviet flag came down over the Kremlin. In about fifteen minutes, this documentary follows the road to that evening: the stagnation of the Brezhnev years, Gorbachev's glasnost and perestroika, the oil-price collapse, the war in Afghanistan, Chernobyl, the Baltic Way, the fall of the Berlin Wall, the August 1991 coup, the Belovezha Accords, and the end of the Soviet Union.
